About ARI Spread Risk Map

This Geographic Information System (GIS) was developed to model and map regional-based Acute Respiratory Infection (ARI) risk levels. By integrating Air Quality Index (AQI) parameters, population density, and weekly active case data, this system assists health agencies in early mitigation and targeted decision-making.

What is the ARI Risk System?

Acute Respiratory Infection (ARI) is an infectious disease attacking any part of the respiratory tract from the nose to the alveoli. Our system combines geographical analysis with air quality parameters (AQI), population density, and weekly active case counts.

Through interactive spatial mapping, health agencies and communities can detect vulnerable regions earlier and take appropriate preventive mitigation steps.

🎯 Main Platform Objectives

  • Map high-risk zones based on air quality & density.
  • Provide independent prediction simulation tools for new regions.
  • Facilitate the export of spatial data and mitigation reports in CSV format.
